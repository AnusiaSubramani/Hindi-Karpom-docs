Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

Hindi Karpom ("the app") helps Tamil-speaking parents understand their child's Hindi homework. This policy explains what information the app collects, how it is used, and how you can request that it be deleted.

Information we collect

  • Account information: When you sign in with Google, we receive your name and email address to create and identify your account.
  • Photos: Photos you take or select are processed entirely on your device using on-device text recognition. Photos are never uploaded to our servers and are not stored by us.
  • Homework text and results: After you review and confirm the Hindi text recognized from a photo, that text - along with the syllable breakdowns, pronunciation guides, and translations generated from it - is stored in our database and linked to your account so you can view your history.

How we use this information

We use the information above solely to provide the app's functionality: authenticating your account, generating syllables/pronunciation/translations, and letting you view your past results.

Sharing with third parties

The Hindi text you confirm (never your photos) is sent to the MyMemory Translation API solely to generate English and Tamil translations. No other personal data is shared with third parties, and we do not sell or use your data for advertising.

Data storage and security

Your account information and homework results are stored using Supabase, with data encrypted in transit. We do not store your photos.

Data deletion

You can request deletion of your account and all associated data at any time by emailing hindikarpomsupport@gmail.com. We will delete your account information and stored homework results; some information may be retained briefly where required for security or legal purposes.

Children's privacy

Hindi Karpom is designed to be used by parents to help with their children's homework. The app does not knowingly collect personal information directly from children.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last updated" date.

Contact us

If you have questions about this policy or your data, contact us at hindikarpomsupport@gmail.com.
