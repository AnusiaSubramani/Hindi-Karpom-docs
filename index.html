About Our Application

Hindi Karpom is an educational application designed to help users learn and master the Hindi language easily. The app provides structured language exercises, vocabulary builders, and interactive learning tools directly on your device.

Google OAuth Usage

Our application uses Google Authentication securely to verify user identity and create a personalized learning profile. We do not access, store, or share your private Google data with any third parties.
